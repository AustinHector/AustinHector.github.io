Setting up

Where to put the phone. Prop it on a small tripod or your bag, directly behind you, looking down the target line. About 10 feet back on the main lens, about 5 feet on the ultrawide. The dashed framing guide on the camera screen shows where to stand; put your feet on the line.

It hears the strike. The microphone catches the click of the ball. The camera is already recording, so the swing is trimmed from your takeaway to your finish and replays on screen. If a swing is missed, tap the red button after it.

Common questions

Nothing replays when I hit. Check that the phone is within a few feet of the ball and that Listening shows at the top of the screen. Raise Sensitivity in settings if strikes are quiet, for example on a mat indoors.

It replays when my neighbor hits. Lower Sensitivity. The app also ignores strikes when nobody in frame swung, so keep yourself inside the framing guide.

The replay starts late or cuts off. Adjust Before takeaway and Finish hold under Trimming in settings, and hold your finish for a moment.

Where are my clips? In the Library. Turn on Keep swings to save every clip, or tap Save on a replay to keep one. Copy to Photos writes a trimmed copy to your Photos library.

Round mode and putts. Putts are quiet, so use Capture putt on the hole screen and tap the button after each one. Every shot is kept and tagged to its hole.

Subscription. Round Replay Pro renews through your Apple account. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Practice mode is free and stays free.
