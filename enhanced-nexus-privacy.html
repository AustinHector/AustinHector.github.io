Enhanced Nexus Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Enhanced Nexus is an unofficial browser extension for authorized users of the SimpleBills Nexus application. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how that information is used.

Information handled locally

Enhanced Nexus operates on supported Nexus pages and Amazon QuickSight reports embedded by Nexus. To provide its user-facing features, the extension may locally process:

  • Nexus page structure and visible website content so it can apply layout and visual improvements.
  • Nexus page addresses and links selected through the Link Sweep feature.
  • Text inside supported Nexus note editors so it can calculate editor height, word count, and character count.
  • Extension settings such as whether Space Grey mode, layout stabilization, scroll protection, enhanced editors, or Link Sweep are enabled.

Storage and retention

Feature preferences are stored locally through Chrome's extension storage and remain until the user changes them or removes the extension.

When deferred Link Sweep loading is enabled, a selected Nexus destination may be held temporarily in Chrome session storage until that tab is opened or closed. Session values are not retained as a browsing history by Enhanced Nexus.

Enhanced Nexus does not store Nexus page content, note contents, account information, authentication information, or customer records.

Data transmission and sharing

Enhanced Nexus does not transmit Nexus page content, note text, browsing activity, extension settings, or personal information to the developer or to any external analytics, advertising, or data service.

The extension does not sell user data. It does not use user data for advertising, credit decisions, or purposes unrelated to its stated functionality.

Nexus and embedded Amazon QuickSight reports continue to communicate with their own services as they normally would. Enhanced Nexus does not replace or modify the privacy practices of those services.

Permissions

Enhanced Nexus requests access only to supported Nexus pages and the specific Amazon QuickSight embed path used by Nexus reports. Chrome storage permission is used for local feature preferences and temporary session-based navigation described above.

Remote code

All executable extension code is included in the Chrome Web Store package. Enhanced Nexus does not download or execute remote code.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of the policy will identify the latest revision.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the support contact shown on the Enhanced Nexus Chrome Web Store listing.
