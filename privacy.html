Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

RoundReplay records your golf swing so it can replay it to you. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes. The short version: everything stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What the app uses

Camera and microphone. The app records video and audio while a Practice or Round session is open, to capture your swing and to hear the ball being struck. Recordings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage.

Photos. If you turn on Copy to Photos, or export a replay, the app writes a copy of that video to your Photos library. It never reads your existing photos or videos.

Location. In Round mode, with your permission, the app uses your location while the hole screen is open to show where you are on the hole and the distance to the green, and stores one location point per shot alongside that shot. To find the course, it sends your approximate position once to OpenStreetMap's public map service and downloads that course's layout, which is then kept on your device. Location is never used in Practice mode and never leaves the phone otherwise.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your videos, audio, or location to any server. The one exception is the course lookup above, which sends an approximate position to OpenStreetMap and nothing else.
  • We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind in the app.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or account information. The app has no accounts.

Subscriptions

Round Replay Pro is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple. Apple handles the purchase, billing, and your payment details; the app only learns whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a clip or a replay, the app hands the video to the iOS share sheet. What happens after that is governed by the app or service you share it with.

Deleting your data

Delete clips and rounds inside the app's Library, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Copies you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are yours to delete from those places.

Children

RoundReplay is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
